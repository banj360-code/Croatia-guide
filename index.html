Getting Around

✈️ Airport Transfers (SPU)

Pre-booked private transfers to Brela cost ~€90–110. Alternatively, take the Airport Shuttle to Split Central Bus Station (€8), then the Arriva/Promet intercity coach south to Brela (€7).

⚠️ The Magistrala Gotcha

Intercity buses drop you at the 'Kričak' stop on the D8 highway above town. Be warned: it is an 8-minute walk down 84 steep stairs to reach the coast. Always book local taxis for door-to-door drops at Hotel Berulia reception.

⛰️ Biokovo Shadow Clock

Because the massive 1,762m Biokovo mountain sits directly behind the coast, direct sunlight vanishes from the beaches earlier than sunset, casting cool natural shade across the coves in the late afternoon.

🌬️ The Adriatic Winds

Maestral: Friendly afternoon sea breeze from the northwest. Ideal for lounging and cooling the pine coves.

Bura: Fierce, gusty mountain wind blowing out to sea. Cancels boat trips and ferries immediately.

Logistics & Essentials

🚻 Toilets & Showers

Clean municipal toilets are spaced evenly along the Lungomare promenade. Beach showers require 50-cent Euro coins to operate (~30 seconds of freshwater).

⚕️ Medical & Pharmacy

Nearest Pharmacy: Ljekarna Baška Voda (1.8km south along the flat promenade). English-speaking staff; stocks sea urchin tweezers, rehydration salts, and antihistamines.

Urgent Care: Dom Zdravlja Makarska (12km south). Open 24/7 for walk-in medical emergencies.
